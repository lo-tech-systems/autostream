# Help & Support

## Start with Troubleshooting

Most problems people run into (can't reach the appliance, speakers not showing up, playback dropping out) are covered in [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md). Check there first.

## Reporting a problem

If Troubleshooting doesn't fix it, or you've found something that looks like a bug, file an issue on the project's GitHub repository:

**https://github.com/lo-tech-systems/autostream/issues**

## What to include

A report is much easier to act on if it has:

- **Component versions.** Open [Info](INFO.md) → **System** and note the autostream build, OS build, and the version/state of each service. Include these in your report.
- **What happened, and how to reproduce it.** What you did, what you expected, and what actually happened. Include steps to reproduce if you can.
- **A log bundle.** See the [Getting logs](TROUBLESHOOTING.md#getting-logs) section of Troubleshooting for the ways to pull logs off the appliance, and attach the resulting ZIP to your report.

## Before you share anything

Logs and screenshots can include your Wi-Fi network name, hostnames, or other details about your home network. Check them over and remove anything private before posting them publicly or attaching them to an issue.
